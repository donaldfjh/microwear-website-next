## 執行的優化操作

### 1. 首頁 (`app/page.tsx`)
- **Title 優化**：`"Microwear | Smartwatch & AI Glasses OEM Manufacturer China | Official Site"` → `"Microwear — Official Site | Smartwatch & AI Glasses OEM Manufacturer China"`（品牌詞前置）
- **Description 優化**：更精煉，強調 "Microwear official website"
- **Keywords 擴充**：新增 `Microwear smart watches`, `Microwear wearable`, `Microwear smartwatch manufacturer`, `Microwear AI glasses manufacturer`
- **Hero H1 優化**：`"Microwear | OEM Smartwatch Manufacturer & AI Glasses Factory"` → `"Microwear — Smartwatch & AI Glasses OEM Manufacturer"`（品牌詞前置）
- **新增 WebSite Schema + SearchAction**：添加 JSON-LD WebSite 結構化數據，啟用 Google 站內搜索框富媒體結果
- **內部連結強化**：SEO 文本區段新增指向 `/products` 和 `/ai-glasses` 的錨文本連結

### 2. 產品頁 (`app/products/page.tsx`)

- **Keywords 擴充**：新增 `microwear smartwatch OEM`, `microwear smartwatch bulk`, `microwear smartwatch supplier`
